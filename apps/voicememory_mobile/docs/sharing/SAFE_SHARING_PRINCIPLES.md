# Safe sharing principles

ArchiveMe may help someone **explain patterns they already saved** to a person they trust. Sharing is optional, explicit, and user-controlled.

## Principles

1. **Opt-in only** — Nothing is shared without a deliberate user action.
2. **Explicit selection** — The user chooses what report, range, or sections are included.
3. **Private by default** — No public profiles, feeds, or discoverable archives.
4. **Reversible** — Future sharing must support revoke and removal where technically possible.
5. **Evidence, not advice** — Shared material summarizes saved moments; it is not care guidance.
6. **No medical role** — ArchiveMe does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional support.

## Approved framing

- Share **only what you choose**
- A **private report** may help explain a **pattern** to someone you trust
- **Future** sharing should be explicit, private, and reversible
- ArchiveMe compares **saved moments over time** — not a chat transcript

## Adjacent audiences (future-safe language)

| Audience | Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family member | "someone you trust" | "family therapy" |
| Coach | "coach" with future/may framing | "coach dashboard", "coach mode" as live |
| Professional support | "professional support" (disclaimer only) | "therapist", "clinical report", "care plan" |

## Privacy defaults (when sharing ships)

1. No transcript in analytics or share URLs by default
2. User previews exactly what will be included
3. Clear label: **this is not a medical record**
4. No automatic re-share when new entries are saved
5. Export handoff (user sends via their own channel) remains distinct from in-app sharing

## Differentiation

Chat products share **conversation threads**. ArchiveMe would share **selected evidence over time** — if and when the feature ships. Until then, all product copy must say **future** or **planned**.
