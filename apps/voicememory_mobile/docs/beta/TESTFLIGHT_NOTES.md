# ArchiveMe — TestFlight notes for testers

Use this as the basis for TestFlight “What to Test” copy and onboarding messages.

---

## What ArchiveMe is

ArchiveMe is a private archive that helps you notice what keeps repeating across moments you save in your own words. Over a few days it may show a repeat, a change, or something that did not come back — always as evidence from your entries, not as a diagnosis.

ArchiveMe is **not** a medical device, therapy app, or mental-health service. It does not provide medical advice, clinical assessment, or treatment.

---

## Suggested TestFlight note

> ArchiveMe helps you notice what keeps repeating across saved moments. For this beta, please record one real moment per day for 3 days, then check whether the app shows a repeat, a change, or something that did not come back.

---

## What testers should try

1. **Fresh install** on a physical iPhone if possible.
2. **Record one real moment per day for 3 days** — voice or typed entry.
3. Use the same real-life thread when it naturally comes back (work pressure, saying yes when you have no capacity, checking when uncertain, etc.).
4. After day 2–3, open **Patterns** and any review prompts that appear.
5. Try **Settings → Testing ArchiveMe? → Send feedback** when something is unclear or broken.
6. Restart the app once to confirm your saved moments are still there.

See [THREE_DAY_TEST_SCRIPT.md](./THREE_DAY_TEST_SCRIPT.md) for a day-by-day script.

---

## Known limitations (beta)

- **Local-first:** entries stay on your device in this beta build.
- **No cloud sync yet** — reinstalling may lose data unless you export/backup.
- **Push notifications may be limited or off** in early TestFlight builds.
- **Value may take 2–3 real entries** on the same thread before repeat/proof surfaces appear.
- ArchiveMe **may not detect every pattern** — absence of a card does not mean nothing is happening.
- **Pro / purchases** may be unavailable or inert on TestFlight until store setup is complete.
- **Restore purchases** should be visible and safe to tap; it may not restore anything in beta.

Full list: [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](./KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).

## What feedback to send

Send via **Settings → Testing ArchiveMe? → Send feedback** (email to `hello@careosapp.co.uk`).

Include:

| Topic | What helps |
| --- | --- |
| **First impression** | Did Record and the first save make sense? |
| **Day 2–3 payoff** | Did a repeat, change, or quiet signal feel accurate — fit, partly fit, or not fit? |
| **Confusing copy** | Quote the **card title or summary** (not your private journal unless you choose to). |
| **Bugs** | Crashes, blank screens, stuck save, mic permission, overflow/layout issues. Device model + iOS version. |
| **Missing step** | Which day/script step failed and how many entries you had. |
| **Restore / Pro** | If you tapped Restore purchases or saw upgrade copy — what happened? |

## Not medical / therapy advice

ArchiveMe uses cautious language (“may”, “might”, “could”) and shows evidence from your own words. It does **not**:

- diagnose conditions or disorders  
- prescribe treatment or therapy  
- score your mental health or wellbeing  
- replace professional care  

If you need medical or mental-health support, contact a qualified professional or local emergency services.
