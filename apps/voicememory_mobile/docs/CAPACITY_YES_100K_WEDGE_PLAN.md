# ArchiveMe — Capacity Yes £100k/month Wedge Plan

Commercial validation pack for the **primary** wedge — not a new product feature. Builds on [WEDGE_RETENTION_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md](./WEDGE_RETENTION_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md) (#140).

## Primary wedge

**For overcommitted professionals who keep saying yes when they have no capacity, ArchiveMe shows the repeated pattern before it costs them.**

**Short version:** Stop guessing why you keep overcommitting. Build the private evidence.

**Positioning:** ArchiveMe is a private evidence archive for repeated overcommitment patterns.

## Target user

Founders, freelancers, managers, senior operators, consultants, and carers/parents under work pressure — people who repeatedly say yes, overdeliver, then feel the cost later.

## Pain statement

- Says yes too quickly
- Takes on too much before checking room
- Cannot see the repeated pattern until after the cost lands
- Feels responsible for keeping things together
- Wants **proof** from their own words, not generic advice
- Wants private reflection without wellness-app branding

## Why this wedge can pay

Overcommitted professionals already spend on tools, coaching-adjacent products, and time recovery. If ArchiveMe clearly surfaces **what keeps repeating** before the next yes, £10–£30/month is plausible for a private long-term evidence trail — especially vs. losing a weekend or a client relationship to another overcommit.

## Why this is sharper than journaling

Journaling captures moments. ArchiveMe **compares** them: what repeated, what changed, what to watch next — after 3+ saves, Then vs Now, and weekly review. The job is pattern evidence, not blank-page writing.

## Why this is safer than wellness positioning

No scores, no labels, no advice to fix you. Cautious reads from your own archive. Not an AI journal, productivity suite, diary, or coaching product.

## 1,000-user growth path

1. **100 users:** validate wedge copy + 3-moment task (organic + founder network)
2. **300 users:** double down on best channel (LinkedIn or short-form)
3. **1,000 users:** App Store listing live, referral loop from beta invite pack, cohort tracking
4. **Path to 3,334+ payers:** requires day-7 retention + annual option + sandbox billing proven — not before

## What must be true for £100k/month

- Clear **capacity yes** resonance in user words (not founder assumption)
- 3-moment task completes at scale
- Day-7 return without streak guilt mechanics
- Then vs Now / weekly review used by engaged segment
- £9.99–£14.99 WTP cluster in interviews
- RevenueCat + sandbox purchase/restore verified
- CAC sustainable on best channel (TBD after 100-user test)

## What would kill the idea

- Users save once and never return
- Insight reads feel generic by moment 3
- Privacy fear blocks save
- WTP clusters at £0 — “nice but not worth paying”
- Prove-enough wedge outperforms capacity yes in same cohort
- Paid ads CAC >> LTV at any tested price

## No-go signals

- Fewer than 3/20 users complete 3 moments
- Zero WTP yes/maybe in first 20 interviews
- Repeated feedback: “I don’t know what to save”
- Users want task management, not pattern evidence
- Any privacy leak in share/calendar/milestone surfaces
