## One-line promise

Save the moment. See the pattern.

## Target user

Overcommitted professionals — founders, managers, consultants, freelancers — who keep saying yes before checking capacity.

## Pain

Says yes too quickly, takes on too much, only sees the cost later, wants private proof from their own words.

## Alternative they use today

Notes app, voice memos, scattered journals — moments saved but not compared across weeks.

## Why ArchiveMe is different

Compares saved moments over time: what repeated, what changed, what to watch next — cautious evidence, not generic advice or task management.

## First 10 seconds

User should understand: *This helps me see why I keep saying yes when I have no room — by saving real moments privately.*

## First 3 moments

1. Save a yes moment (or when the cost landed)
2. Save two more real moments
3. Open Archive — see whether the same pattern shows up in their words

## Day 7 reason to return

Then vs Now or weekly review when eligible — compare earlier vs recent saves before the next yes.

## Why someone might pay

Long-term private evidence trail, export, deeper compare history — worth £9.99–£14.99/month if moment 3 + return prove useful.

## What not to say

AI journal, therapy app, coaching, productivity suite, wellness scores, fake urgency, invented user counts, “ArchiveMe knows you,” Buy now / Subscribe now.
