## Broad product

**ArchiveMe shows what keeps repeating in your life.**

## Primary launch wedge

**Start with one pattern: saying yes when you have no capacity.**

## One-line promise

Save the moment. See the pattern.

## Audience

**People who keep taking on too much, agreeing too quickly, or only seeing the cost later.**

**Examples (not the whole audience):** Founders, managers, freelancers, carers, parents, students, and anyone who keeps overcommitting.

## Pain

Says yes too quickly, takes on too much, only sees the cost later, wants private proof from their own words.

## Alternative they use today

Notes app, voice memos, scattered saves — moments stored but not compared across weeks.

## Why ArchiveMe is different

Compares saved moments: what repeated, what changed, what to watch next — cautious evidence from your archive, not generic advice.

## First 10 seconds

User should understand: *ArchiveMe shows what keeps repeating — I'm starting with the yes-with-no-capacity pattern.*

## First 3 moments

1. Save a yes moment (or when the cost landed)
2. Save two more real moments
3. Open Archive — see whether the same pattern shows up

## Day 7 reason to return

Then vs Now or weekly review — compare earlier vs recent saves before the next yes; broader archive grows from there.

## Why someone might pay

Long-term private evidence trail, export, deeper compare history — worth £9.99–£14.99/month if moment 3 + return prove useful.

## What not to say

AI journal, wellness app, coaching, productivity suite, “busy professionals only,” fake urgency, invented user counts, hard purchase CTAs before paid launch.
