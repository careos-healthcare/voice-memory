## Review notes for this wedge

- Emphasize **pattern evidence** and **privacy**
- Do not claim outcomes (“fix overcommitment”, “reduce stress clinically”)
- Beta builds: Pro Preview / sandbox only — no live purchase claims
- Screenshots must not show real personal content
- Align with [CAPACITY_YES_LANDING_PAGE_COPY.md](./CAPACITY_YES_LANDING_PAGE_COPY.md)

## Privacy positioning (listing)

- Entries stored on device; user controls export
- Optional PIN / biometrics via Security settings
- Share and milestone surfaces use fixed copy and counts — not raw transcript text
- No account required to start saving moments

## Long description (test)

ArchiveMe is a private evidence archive for what keeps repeating in your life.

**Start with one pattern:** saying yes when you have no capacity. Save a moment when you agree — or when the cost shows up later. After a few saves, compare what keeps repeating, what changed, and what to watch next. Cautious reads come from your own words — not generic advice.

Features:
• Voice or typed moments
• Archive Home with pattern-oriented summary when eligible
• Then vs Now comparison
• Weekly review ritual when eligible
• Share-safe proof (counts only)
• Optional app lock

Not a task manager or wellness score app. For people who take on too much — founders, managers, students, carers, and anyone who keeps overcommitting — who want private proof before the next yes.
