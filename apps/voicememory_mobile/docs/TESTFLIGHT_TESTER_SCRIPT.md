# ArchiveMe — TestFlight tester script

Short script for internal testers. Aim for **10 minutes** on a physical iPhone with a **fresh install** if possible.

---

## What ArchiveMe is (one sentence)

**ArchiveMe is a private archive that watches what keeps repeating in your own words and, over time, shows what changed and what may have helped.**

---

## First 10 minutes — try this

1. **Open ArchiveMe** and skip or finish onboarding.
2. **Save your first moment** on Record — voice or **Type instead** (mic not required).
3. Open **Patterns** and confirm you see a calm empty or first-saved state (not a wall of mature tools).
4. **Save a second moment** on the same theme you might notice again (see journey below).
5. Glance at **Settings** — find **Testing ArchiveMe? → Send feedback** (you can use this later).
6. Do **not** worry about Pro, restore, or every card — focus on Record + Patterns first.

---

## 6-entry proof journey

Use one real thread you might say again (example: agreeing when you have no capacity, checking when uncertain, pressure before saying yes). **Same theme across entries 1–3** is what makes the proof appear.

| Step | What to save | What you should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. First recording** | One honest moment in your own words. | Receipt that your moment was saved — **not** a full pattern yet. |
| **2. Second related recording** | Same kind of moment again (similar wording/situation). | Early signal that something **may** be repeating — cautious, not diagnostic. |
| **3. Third related recording** | Same thread a third time. | **Confirmed repeat** with short evidence — still evidence-led, not a diagnosis. |
| **4. Trigger recording** | When prompted, record **what happened right before** it came up (not just the feeling). | Trigger payoff / timeline adds a “what may have started it” step. |
| **5. Softer return** | When the same thread returns, note if it felt **less urgent** or easier to stop. | Change / softening notice — calm “something changed this time” copy. |
| **6. Helpful action** | If prompted, record **what helped** (e.g. paused, asked for help, waited before responding). | Helpful-action step on the timeline — what may have softened the loop. |

**Tips**

- Entries can be **typed** — faster for testing.
- You do not need all six in one sitting; spread over a day or two is fine.
- If a step does not appear, note **which step** and **how many entries** you had — that is useful feedback.
- Tap **See how ArchiveMe works** on Patterns if you want a sample timeline before you have real proof.

---

## What to report back

Send feedback via **Settings → Testing ArchiveMe? → Send feedback** (opens email to `hello@careosapp.co.uk`).

Please include:

| Topic | What we need |
| --- | --- |
| **Clear / confusing** | What made sense in the first 10 minutes; what felt vague or surprising. |
| **Emotional moment** | Any line that felt accurate, unfair, or too strong — quote the **card title or summary**, not your private journal text unless you choose to. |
| **Bugs** | Crashes, blank screens, stuck save, mic permission issues, sync errors. Device model + iOS version. |
| **Paywall confusion** | If Pro / upgrade copy appeared — was it clear purchases may be unavailable on TestFlight? |
| **Restore purchase issue** | If you tapped **Restore purchases** — what happened? (Purchases may be unavailable until store setup is complete.) |
| **Feedback email worked** | Did **Send feedback** open your mail app with the template? If not, say what you saw. |

**Honest feedback beats polite feedback.** Skip cards that do not apply to you.

---

## Do not test as success criteria (this build)

- Buying Pro (may be unavailable on TestFlight)
- Clinical, wellness, or mental-health claims — ArchiveMe is an evidence archive, not therapy
- Every Settings or archive tool — focus on Record, Patterns, and the 6-step journey above
