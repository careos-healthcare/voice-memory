# ArchiveMe — TestFlight Beta Launch Plan (Capacity Yes Wedge)

Docs-only readiness pack for the next TestFlight cohort. No new product features in this branch.

## Goal

Test whether users understand and complete the capacity-yes wedge:

**Catch the yes before it costs you → Save a yes moment → See what pulled you in → Review what changed → Tell us if it fits.**

This beta is evidence for wedge clarity and completion — not for paid conversion yet.

## Core promise

**Catch the yes before it costs you.**

First-session path:

1. Save a yes moment
2. See what pulled you in
3. Review what changed
4. Tell us if it fits

## Beta mission (tester-facing)

Use ArchiveMe for 7 days. Save 3 yes moments. Review your yes loop. Tell us if it fits.

## Target cohort

- **20 beta users** invited to TestFlight
- People who recognize saying yes when they have no capacity
- iOS device with TestFlight installed
- Willing to save real moments (voice or typed) and do one short follow-up

## Success targets

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Invited | **20** |
| Save first yes moment | **10** |
| Save 3 yes moments | **5** |
| Return by day 7 | **3** |
| Say loop fits or partly fits | **3** |
| Say they would pay | **2** |

Track in a simple spreadsheet or notes — no backend analytics required for this cohort.

## What to observe

- Do testers understand the headline in the first 30 seconds?
- Do they save a real yes moment without over-explaining?
- Does the 3-moment path feel clear on Archive Home?
- Does the yes loop feel accurate after 3 saves?
- Where do they drop off (install, first save, third save, loop review, fit check)?

## What not to test yet

- Paid subscriptions or RevenueCat checkout
- Broad journaling or generic archive positioning
- Wellness or mental-health framing
- Generic productivity or coaching positioning
- Streaks, scores, or guilt-based retention
