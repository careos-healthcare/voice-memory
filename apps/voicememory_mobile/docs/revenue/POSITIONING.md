# ArchiveMe — Positioning

ArchiveMe is a **private evidence archive** for what keeps returning in your life — built from moments you save in your own words, compared across days and weeks.

It is **not** a chatbot, journal app, or wellness coach.

---

## The contrast

| | Generic AI chat | ArchiveMe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Job** | Answer today’s question | Show what keeps returning over time |
| **Input** | Prompt | Real moment (voice or typed) |
| **Output** | Advice, ideas, conversation | Evidence, repeat, change, quiet |
| **Memory** | Session or thread | Local archive with proof gates |
| **Tone** | Helpful assistant | Cautious witness (“may”, not “you should”) |

**ChatGPT answers today. ArchiveMe shows the timeline behind the pattern.**

---

## What ArchiveMe is

- An **evidence archive**, not a chat  
- A **pattern memory** built from your entries, not generic coaching  
- A **return loop**: save → come back → notice repeat → confirm truth → see change → preserve what mattered  

ArchiveMe should feel like: *“This remembered what I actually said — and showed me when it came back.”*

---

## Core loop

```
Record → Return → Notice repeat → Prove change → Preserve evidence
```

| Stage | User action | ArchiveMe job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Record** | Save one real moment | Capture without homework tone |
| **Return** | Come back next day / same thread | Watch prompt, return check |
| **Notice repeat** | Third related moment (typical) | First proof, early signal |
| **Prove change** | Answer truth / what changed | Evidence-led cards, not diagnosis |
| **Preserve evidence** | Correction, rename, export (Pro) | Longer memory, private report |

Monetization attaches to **preserve** — longer history, timeline, export — not to “better answers.”

---

## What to avoid

Generic coaching language undermines differentiation:

- “Here’s what you should do”  
- “Let’s reflect on your journey”  
- Streak guilt, mood scores, wellness dashboards  
- “AI therapist” or clinical framing  
- Competing with ChatGPT on answer quality  

Prefer: **your words**, **what returned**, **what changed**, **what got quieter**.

---

## One-line positioning options

Use for App Store, landing hero tests, and investor one-liners.

1. **“Stop losing the patterns you keep noticing.”**  
2. **“Your personal evidence archive for what keeps returning.”**  
3. **“See what changed, what returned, and what finally got quieter.”**  
4. **“Not another journal. A memory for your patterns.”**

**Recommended default (hero test A):** #1 — problem-led, distinct from “AI assistant.”

**Recommended subhero:** #3 — explains payoff without claiming therapy outcomes.

---

## Audience wedge (current beta)

Primary wedge in copy and onboarding: **saying yes when you have no capacity** — but the product must work for any repeating thread the user saves.

Positioning stays **thread-agnostic**; wedge is **acquisition**, not product definition.

---

## Safety positioning

ArchiveMe is for personal reflection. It is **not** medical or mental-health advice and must not imply diagnosis or treatment.

See `docs/beta/TESTFLIGHT_NOTES.md` for tester-facing safety wording.
