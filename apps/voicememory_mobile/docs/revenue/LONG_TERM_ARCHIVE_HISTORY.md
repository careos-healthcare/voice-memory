# ArchiveMe — Long-term archive history

**Purpose:** Historical product note, updated to defer commercial policy to
`../../MONETIZATION_CONTRACT.md` and the canonical entitlement matrix.

---

## Product job

ArchiveMe compares **moments you saved at different times**. Long-term history is the moat:

- What **returned** on another day
- What **changed** between entries
- What **softened** or got **quieter**
- What **helped** when you named it

ChatGPT does not preserve your real moments this way. A generic journal does not compare with evidence gates.

---

## Free vs Pro (messaging)

| Tier | History framing | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Every original moment remains accessible; the first valid observation and first valid comparison are included | Canonical |
| **Pro** | New ongoing comparisons, full Changes history generation, and deeper archive synthesis | Canonical |

**Honesty:** Pro may gate new costly generation, never original history,
playback, editing, search, export, or deletion.

---

## Surfaces that express long-term value

| Surface | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Change timeline | Day-by-day pattern movement |
| Pattern map / archive beliefs | Cross-entry view of what returned |
| Weekly / monthly review | Revisit older proof |
| Search key moments | Find older evidence |
| Private report | Summarize longer arc |

All are **local archive intelligence** — not a chat transcript export.

---

## Copy guidelines

**Use:**

- “Keep the longer story”
- “Older evidence stays available”
- “See what returned across weeks”
- “Your archive grows; Pro keeps more of it”

**Avoid:**

- “Infinite memory”
- “Automatic cloud backup” (not live)
- “Sync across all devices” (future — docs only)
- “AI remembers everything for you”
