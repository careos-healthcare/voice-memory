# ArchiveMe — Capacity Yes Beta Interview Script

Structured interviews for the **launch wedge** (saying yes with no capacity) on a **broad private archive**. **Stay in pattern-evidence language.** Record notes; do not quote users publicly without consent.

**Broad product:** ArchiveMe shows what keeps repeating in your life. **Bridge:** Start with one pattern: saying yes when you have no capacity.

## Screening questions

1. What describes you best (founder, freelancer, manager, student, carer/parent, other)?
2. In the last month, did you agree to something before checking if you had capacity?
3. How often does the cost of that yes show up later (same week, next week)?
4. Do you already use notes, voice memos, or journals for work stress?
5. Are you willing to save 3 real moments in ArchiveMe over 14 days and do one 15-minute follow-up?
6. iOS TestFlight available on your phone?

**Include if:** repeated yes-with-no-capacity pain, willing to save honestly, can complete 3 moments.

**Exclude if:** seeking clinical support, wants task management only, cannot install TestFlight.

## Onboarding interview (day 0, ~10 min)

1. What made you interested in testing an overcommitment archive?
2. Describe the last time you said yes when you probably had no capacity.
3. What did you do after — note, vent, nothing?
4. What would “useful” look like after a few saves?
5. Any privacy concerns about voice or text on device?
6. Walk through install → first save (live or async).

**Exact question (first moment context):**  
“What made you save the first moment?”

## After first moment (~24–48h, ~5 min)

1. What made you save the first moment?
2. Was voice or type faster for you?
3. Anything confusing on Record or Archive Home?
4. Did you think about saving again when another yes happened?

## After third moment (~day 3–7, ~15 min)

1. What made you save the first moment?
2. **Did ArchiveMe show anything you did not expect?**
3. By moment 3, could you describe what might be repeating — in your words?
4. **Would you open this before saying yes next time?**
5. Did Then vs Now or weekly review add anything?
6. What felt generic vs specific?
7. **Would you pay to keep a long-term archive of this pattern?**
8. **At what price would this feel too expensive?**
9. **What would make this a must-keep app?**

## Day-3 interview (~5 min)

1. Have you returned since first save?
2. How many moments saved so far?
3. Anything blocking a third save?
4. One change that would help you finish the 3-moment task?

## Day-7 interview (~15 min)

1. Total moments saved; last open date.
2. Did ArchiveMe show anything you did not expect?
3. Would you open this before saying yes next time?
4. Retention: what would bring you back next week without guilt or streaks?
5. Willingness to pay (see below).
6. Would you recommend to one colleague with same pain? Why or why not?

## Willingness-to-pay questions

1. Would you pay to keep a long-term archive of this pattern?
2. At what price would this feel **cheap** / a obvious yes?
3. At what price would this feel **fair** for monthly use?
4. **At what price would this feel too expensive?**
5. Would you prefer monthly (£6.99 / £9.99 / £14.99) or annual (£79 / £99 / £149)?
6. What would make this a must-keep app?
7. What would make you delete it?

## Pricing sensitivity questions

1. Show ladder: £6.99, £9.99, £14.99, £19.99/month — which feels right for **your** use?
2. What feature would justify £14.99 vs £9.99? (export, longer archive, Then vs Now, app lock)
3. If it stayed free but capped at 10 moments, would you pay to unlock unlimited?
4. Compare to what you already pay (notes app, cloud storage, professional tools).

## Retention questions

1. What would bring you back weekly without a streak?
2. Which screen do you open first — Record or Archive?
3. Did Today’s One Question or daily exercise help or distract?
4. What notification (if any) would be welcome — and what would feel spammy?

## Privacy objection questions

1. What worries you about voice or text on device?
2. Did app lock / security settings meet your bar?
3. Would export-only backup increase or decrease trust?
4. Share/milestone cards — comfortable with counts-only proof?

## Competitor comparison questions

1. What do you use today (Notes, Voice Memos, Day One, Notion, other)?
2. Why isn’t that enough for this yes-pattern?
3. Did you try any wellness or coaching apps for this? What missed?
4. Would a task manager solve this — or is the gap **seeing the pattern**?

## Interviewer checklist

- [ ] All exact required questions asked by moment 3 or day 7
- [ ] WTP and “too expensive” price recorded
- [ ] User segment tagged (founder / freelancer / manager / student / carer / other — examples only)
- [ ] Cohort noted (`capacity_yes_direct` if applicable)
- [ ] No leading wellness or diagnostic language
