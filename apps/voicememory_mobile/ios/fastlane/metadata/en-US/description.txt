A private change ledger that shows exactly what repeated, what changed, the words proving it, and lets you correct the record.

See what repeated. See what changed. Verify it in your own words.

Save one real moment in your own words. When something like it happens again, ArchiveMe compares the two and shows the exact saved words and dates behind the comparison.

With ArchiveMe you can:
• Save one real moment by voice or by typing
• Read and edit the saved transcript, and replay your own audio
• See what repeated and what changed across your saved moments
• Open the exact source words and dates proving any comparison
• Correct the record with Accurate, Wrong angle, Too generic, or Hide
• Export your saved moments whenever you choose

ArchiveMe uses AI for transcription and for drafting each observation. That is a processing detail, not the product: nothing is shown without the exact saved words and dates behind it.

ArchiveMe is not therapy, medical advice, or a diagnosis. Some transcription and analysis features may process audio or text when you use them.
