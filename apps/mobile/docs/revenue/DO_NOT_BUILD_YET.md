# ArchiveMe — Do not build yet

Scope guardrails before wider beta and live billing. Items here are **intentionally deferred** — not a backlog commitment.

**Do not modify product code** to ship these during the TestFlight upload wait unless a item moves out via explicit decision + beta evidence.

---

## AI and coaching

| Item | Why wait |
| --- | --- |
| **AI chat** | Collapses differentiation vs ChatGPT; distracts from evidence loop |
| **Generic coaching** | “You should…” tone breaks trust and positioning |
| **GPT-5 synthesis as hero** | Pro intelligence is supplement, not core beta story |
| **Ask ArchiveMe anything** | Wrong product metaphor |

---

## Social and marketplace

| Item | Why wait |
| --- | --- |
| **Social feed** | Violates private archive trust |
| **Groups / shared archives** | Privacy and moderation burden |
| **Marketplace** | No two-sided liquidity; off-mission |
| **Public pattern sharing** | User shame and safety risk |

---

## Clinical and B2B

| Item | Why wait |
| --- | --- |
| **Therapist dashboard** | Regulatory, sales cycle, liability |
| **Clinical positioning** | Not a medical device; beta is consumer reflection |
| **Team / clinician version** | See [NEXT_PRODUCT_BETS.md](./NEXT_PRODUCT_BETS.md) — much later |

---

## Infrastructure (premature)

| Item | Why wait |
| --- | --- |
| **Complex cloud sync** | Prove local loop + WTP first |
| **Notifications campaigns** | `docs/product/notifications-decision.md` — after return proven |
| **Multi-device backup** | Marked **Future** in Pro ladder |
| **Server entitlement anti-sharing** | Needs stable SKU + identity model |

---

## Product complexity

| Item | Why wait |
| --- | --- |
| **Too many pattern engines** | Confuses review inbox and tester comprehension |
| **Complicated subscriptions** | Multiple tiers, add-ons, family plans |
| **Habit streak gamification** | Wrong retention mechanic for ArchiveMe |
| **Mood scores / wellness dashboards** | Wrong category; regulatory tone risk |
| **New proof thresholds / evidence gates** | Protected — learn from beta before changing |

---

## Monetization mistakes

| Item | Why wait |
| --- | --- |
| **Paywall on day 0** | No proof → no trust → bad reviews |
| **Lifetime SKU without cap** | Support and revenue predictability |
| **Competing on ChatGPT Plus price** | Wrong comparison frame |
| **Fake “Pro active” on TestFlight** | See `docs/ACCESS_PROTECTION_AUDIT.md` |
