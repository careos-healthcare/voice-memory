## How it works

**Section title:** Built for return, not for chatting.

1. **Record one real moment** — voice or typed. No homework tone.  
2. **Come back tomorrow** — did the same thread return, change, or stay quiet?  
3. **See first proof** — after a few related moments, ArchiveMe shows evidence from *your* words.  
4. **Confirm what fits** — yes, sort of, or no. ArchiveMe adjusts.  
5. **Keep the memory** — Pro preserves full history, timeline, and export.

**Section title:** Not another answer engine.

## What first proof looks like

**Section title:** Proof from your life, not generic coaching.

After you save a few related moments, ArchiveMe may ask:

- **“Does this feel true?”** — with snippets from what you actually said.  
- **Not** a diagnosis. **Not** “you should try…”  
- Cautious language: *may*, *might*, *could*.

**Visual suggestion:** blurred card mock — title + two evidence snippets + three-button truth check.

**Disclaimer:** ArchiveMe is not medical or therapy advice.
