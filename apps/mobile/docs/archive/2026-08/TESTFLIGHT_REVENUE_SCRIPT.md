# ArchiveMe — TestFlight revenue interview script

Questions for 15–20 minute tester calls or async feedback. Use **after** the [3-day test script](../beta/THREE_DAY_TEST_SCRIPT.md).

**Goal:** learn differentiation and willingness to pay — not sell Pro on a broken TestFlight build.

Do not change billing or paywall code based on a single interview.

---

## Setup (30 seconds)

- “This is research, not a sales call.”  
- “Purchases may not work on TestFlight — we’re learning what would be worth paying for.”  
- “You can skip anything that didn’t apply to your week.”  

---

## Core questions

Ask in order; follow up with “why?” once per section.

### 1. Truth and value

**Did ArchiveMe show you something that felt true?**

- Probe: fit · partly fit · not fit  
- Quote the **card title** they remember, not their private journal unless they offer  

### 2. Differentiation

**Was this different from ChatGPT?**

- Probe: memory vs advice · conversation vs archive  
- If “no”: what would make it different enough?  

### 3. Return intent

**Would you come back tomorrow?**

- Probe: what would pull them back — watch target, curiosity, habit?  
- What would make them **stop** returning?  

---

## Monetization questions

### 4. General WTP

**What would make this worth paying for?**

- Listen for: memory, export, timeline, privacy — not “better AI answers”  

### 5. Longer memory

**Would longer memory matter?**

- Explain: “Free keeps last 7 key moments; Pro keeps full searchable history.”  
- Would that matter **after** they felt first proof?  

### 6. Private report

**Would a private report matter?**

- Probe: personal recap · share with therapist on their terms · legal/work record (do not encourage clinical claims)  

### 7. Export

**Would export matter?**

- PDF · print · send to self · backup fear  

### 8. Price fairness

**What price feels fair?**

Show three options (do not defend them):

- £4.99 / month  
- £39.99 / year  
- £79 one-time founding member  

Ask: fair · expensive · bargain · wouldn’t pay  

Optional: “At what price would it feel like a rip-off?”  

---

## Closing

- “Anything we should **not** build?”  
- Point to Settings → Send feedback for bugs  
- Thank them — honest “wouldn’t pay” is useful
