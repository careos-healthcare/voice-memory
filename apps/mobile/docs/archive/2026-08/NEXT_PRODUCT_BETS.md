# ArchiveMe — Next product bets (ranked)

Future bets after TestFlight revenue interviews and 3-day script results. **Ranked by expected ROI × feasibility** for a solo/small team — re-rank quarterly.

Not a roadmap commitment. See [DO_NOT_BUILD_YET.md](./DO_NOT_BUILD_YET.md) for exclusions.

---

## 1. Pro longer memory conversion

**Bet:** Users who hit first proof will pay for full history + timeline when the 7 key-moment ceiling bites.

**Why #1:** Aligns with `ArchiveProFeatureMap` — monetization already defined; soft gates exist.

**Validate:** Experiment E + WTP on “longer memory” question.

**Risk:** Enforcement gaps — user gets Pro value without paying if gates stay soft.

---

## 2. Private report upgrade

**Bet:** Export-minded users convert on “keep a private copy” more than on abstract Pro.

**Why #2:** Concrete deliverable; differentiates from ChatGPT threads.

**Validate:** Experiment D + export WTP in [TESTFLIGHT_REVENUE_SCRIPT.md](./TESTFLIGHT_REVENUE_SCRIPT.md).

**Risk:** Over-promising clinical “report” language.

---

## 3. Web landing page conversion

**Bet:** “Not ChatGPT” landing (Experiment H) improves qualified TestFlight → retained user ratio.

**Why #3:** Cheap to test; fixes positioning before paid acquisition.

**Validate:** Install → day-3 retention vs hero B/C.

**Risk:** Traffic without product readiness wastes cohort.

---

## 4. Founder beta lifetime offer

**Bet:** £49–£99 capped lifetime funds runway and creates evangelists.

**Why #4:** Cash + feedback; good for first 50–100 users.

**Validate:** Experiment A waitlist + stated WTP.

**Risk:** Support burden; avoid unlimited lifetime without cap.

---

## 5. Multi-device backup

**Bet:** Paying users demand sync once archive exceeds ~30 entries.

**Why #5:** Real need — but **Future** until local loop and billing work.

**Validate:** Interview mentions “lost phone fear” unprompted.

**Risk:** Sync complexity, conflict resolution, trust.

---

## 6. Therapy companion export

**Bet:** “Bring evidence to session” positioning lifts WTP without building clinician tools.

**Why #6:** Copy/export only — no dashboard.

**Validate:** Experiment G messaging test.

**Risk:** Medical adjacency — stay user-controlled export only.

---

## 7. Weekly email / report

**Bet:** Passive return cue increases retention and Pro teaser opens.

**Why #7:** Notifications decision deferred — email is lighter than push.

**Validate:** Opt-in rate if offered post-proof.

**Risk:** Privacy expectations; email as “another newsletter.”

---

## 8. Team / clinician version (much later)

**Bet:** B2B seats for coaches/therapists archiving client-agreed exports.

**Why last:** Long sales cycle, compliance, far from current beta.

**Validate:** Only after ≥ 100 paying consumers and repeated “my therapist asked” quotes.

**Risk:** Product becomes clinical by accident.

---
