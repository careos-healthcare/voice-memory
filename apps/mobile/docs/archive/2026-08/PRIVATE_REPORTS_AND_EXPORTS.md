# ArchiveMe — Private reports and exports

**Purpose:** Clarify Pro value around **reports you keep** — not audio sharing, not clinical summaries, not public posts.

---

## Private report

A **private report** is a recap of what the archive noticed across your saved moments:

- Repeats that crossed the evidence gate
- Change the user confirmed
- Quiet signals that softened
- Timeline context in cautious language

**Rules:**

- Built from **your words**, not generic coaching
- **Private** — not shared by default
- **Not medical** — no diagnosis, treatment, or therapy framing
- Preview on free; **full keep/export** is Pro value when live

---

## Export

Export means **a copy you control** — PDF, markdown, or local backup file — depending on what the build actually ships.

| Export type | Typical status | Consumer copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local backup JSON | Partial / internal | Do not headline in store |
| Print / PDF recap | Partial | “Exportable reports” when live |
| Share sheet to another app | Partial | “Export a copy you control” |
| Auto cloud export | **Future** | Docs only |

**Honesty rule:** If export is not fully Pro-gated and tested, use **“planned”** in in-app copy (`Exportable reports (planned)`) — not in App Store until live.

---

## What exports are NOT

- Sharing raw audio publicly
- Therapist-ready clinical notes
- Automated “send to your coach” (future — see [SAFE_SHARING_STRATEGY.md](./SAFE_SHARING_STRATEGY.md))
- Sync to a server by default

Copy must say: **Only share what you choose.**

---

## Paid reason tie-in

> Pro is for **keeping the evidence** — private reports and exports are how the longer story leaves the app when *you* decide.

Not: “Export to get AI insights.”
