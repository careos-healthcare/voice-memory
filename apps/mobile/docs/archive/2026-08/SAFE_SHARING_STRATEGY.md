# ArchiveMe — Safe sharing strategy

**Purpose:** Plan for family / coach / therapist-**adjacent** sharing without medical claims or live product promises.

---

## Principle

ArchiveMe helps you **talk about patterns with someone you trust** — using evidence you already saved. It does **not** diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.

---

## Approved future-safe copy (consumer)

Use in product copy, docs, and beta interviews:

- **“Private sharing may help you talk about patterns with someone you trust.”**
- **“ArchiveMe does not diagnose or treat anything.”**
- **“Only share what you choose.”**

---

## Banned language (never use)

| Banned | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Therapy, therapist replacement | Medical adjacency |
| Diagnosis, treatment, clinical | Regulatory / trust risk |
| Mental health care, medical advice | Implies licensed care |
| “Share with your therapist automatically” | Overpromise + clinical |
| “Coach mode” as live feature | Not shipped |

**Allowed adjacency:** “someone you trust,” “coach,” “family member” — only with **future** or **may** framing.

---

## Sharing modes (strategy — not live)

| Mode | Description | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Export handoff** | User exports PDF/markdown and sends via their own channel | Partial (export path) |
| **Invite read-only link** | Trusted person views selected report | **Future** |
| **Shared family archive** | Multi-user vault | **Future** |
| **Coach workspace** | Third-party dashboard | **Future — do not build yet** |

All **Future** items stay in strategy docs until beta validates demand. See [DO_NOT_BUILD_YET.md](./DO_NOT_BUILD_YET.md).

---

## Privacy defaults (when sharing ships)

1. Opt-in only — never auto-share entries
2. User selects **which report or date range**
3. No transcript in analytics or share URLs by default
4. Clear revoke / delete path
5. No “social feed” or public profile

---

## Differentiation vs ChatGPT

ChatGPT shares a **conversation thread**. ArchiveMe would share **selected evidence over time** — if and when sharing ships. Until then, say **future** in docs only.
